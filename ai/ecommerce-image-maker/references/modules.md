# A+ Detail Page Module Library

Use these modules to plan Amazon-style or cross-border ecommerce listing images.

## Hero Module

Goal: make the category, product identity, and primary promise clear in one glance.

Use:

- Large product render or realistic scene photo
- Clean headline with product category
- 2 to 3 icon-backed benefits
- Soft commercial lighting
- Lifestyle props that match the product

Avoid:

- Too many text blocks
- Product smaller than props
- Decorative backgrounds unrelated to the buyer scene

## Parameter Icon Strip

Goal: allow fast comparison and scanning.

Use 4 to 6 specs:

- Capacity, power, size, load, material, pressure, speed, battery, compatibility
- Simple line icons
- Short labels

Keep numbers consistent across every image.

## Detail Close-Up Grid

Goal: prove quality and usability.

Use 4 to 6 close-ups:

- Buttons or controls
- Handles and grips
- Ports and connectors
- Removable parts
- Texture and material
- Base, feet, lock, hinge, tank, tray, filter, nozzle

Each close-up needs a short proof phrase, not a paragraph.

## Performance or Structure Module

Goal: show the internal or functional reason behind the claim.

Good for:

- Coffee machines: pressure pump, heating, steam wand, boiler, extraction path
- Fitness equipment: frame thickness, base stability, load-bearing structure
- Appliances: airflow, heating, cooling, filtration, motor, battery

Use transparent cutaway or exploded visual only when it will not misrepresent the product.

## Usage Scenario Module

Goal: show how the product fits real life.

Use:

- Product in a realistic room or environment
- Human hand or partial user interaction if useful
- Props that explain scale and use
- Warm but clean lighting

Avoid changing the product to fit the scene. The scene adapts to the product.

## Size and Fit Module

Goal: answer "will it fit?"

Use:

- Product with dimension lines
- Human silhouette or common objects for scale
- Storage footprint
- Before/after placement when relevant

Keep dimensions exact. If dimensions are unknown, mark them as placeholders instead of inventing numbers.

## Scene Fit Module

Goal: show suitable places or people.

Use:

- 3 to 5 application icons or small scenes
- Home, office, apartment, kitchen, coffee corner, gym, garage, camping, travel

Best for compact products, adjustable products, and products with multiple audiences.

## Comparison Module

Goal: show the advantage over ordinary alternatives.

Use:

- Clear left-right comparison
- 3 to 5 criteria
- Objective claims only

Avoid unverifiable superiority claims unless the user provides evidence.

## Package or Accessory Module

Goal: reduce uncertainty about what is included.

Use:

- Flat lay of product and accessories
- Labels for each item
- Quantity markers

Do not add accessories that are not confirmed.

## Closing Lifestyle Module

Goal: end the page with brand mood and purchase confidence.

Use:

- Product in polished final scene
- Short emotional line
- Minimal copy
- Consistent visual tone with the first hero module
