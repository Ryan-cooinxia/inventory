# Ecommerce Image Workflow

Use this workflow when planning a reusable AI ecommerce image set from product photos and selling points.

## 1. Product Understanding

Start by describing the product as if preparing a product-spec sheet.

Capture:

- Category: what the product is
- Audience: who buys it and why
- Core structure: body, frame, buttons, handles, legs, tubes, tanks, ports, screens, moving parts
- Function points: parts the user touches, adjusts, opens, plugs in, cleans, carries, or stores
- Material and finish: metal, plastic, glass, silicone, fabric, wood, matte, glossy, brushed, transparent
- Parameters: size, capacity, power, weight, load, voltage, pressure, temperature, compatibility
- Usage scenes: home, kitchen, office, garage, gym, car, outdoor, travel, pet, baby, beauty, industrial

Mark anything that must not change. For example: button count, product silhouette, handle angle, port position, tank position, frame proportion, contact points, material split lines.

## 2. Buyer Doubts

Translate product facts into buyer questions. Good ecommerce images answer doubts before the buyer asks.

Common buyer doubts:

- Will it fit my space?
- Is it strong enough?
- Is it safe or stable?
- Is it easy to use?
- Is it easy to clean or store?
- What can it do for me?
- What details prove the quality?
- Is the size, capacity, or compatibility right?
- What comes in the package?

Turn each important doubt into one image topic.

## 3. Selling Point Grouping

Group features into themes. Do not mix unrelated claims.

Example groups:

- Stability/load: frame, base, suction cups, thickness, load number
- Adjustment/fit: height, width, angle, user height, storage size
- Performance: motor, pressure, wattage, heating, battery, speed
- Convenience: detachable, washable, foldable, one-button use, easy assembly
- Scene: home kitchen, apartment, office, coffee corner, gym, camping
- Detail quality: close-ups of material, switch, hinge, tank, nozzle, cable, connector

For each group, write:

- Main claim
- Proof point
- Visual evidence
- Risk if AI changes the product

## 4. Image Set Planning

Plan images from simple to persuasive:

1. Main hero image: product identity and primary benefit
2. Key parameter strip: quick scan of top specs
3. Detail close-ups: prove material and quality
4. Performance/structure image: show why it works
5. Usage scenario: show target lifestyle or workflow
6. Size/fit image: answer space and compatibility
7. Comparison or before/after: clarify advantage
8. Final brand/lifestyle image: leave a polished commercial impression

For small projects, use 5 to 7 images. For A+ detail pages, use 8 to 12 modules.

## 5. Iteration Logic

When reviewing generated images, classify issues:

- Product drift: wrong shape, missing part, wrong count, wrong position
- Parameter drift: inconsistent numbers or impossible scale
- Text drift: misspellings, unreadable labels, too much copy
- Scene drift: props or environment distract from product
- Composition drift: main product too small, crop is awkward, hierarchy unclear

Write revision prompts that fix one issue group at a time.
